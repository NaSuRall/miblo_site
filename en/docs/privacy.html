Privacy and cookies

Choose whether this website uses Google Analytics, and understand what it measures.

Your choice

Google Analytics measures this website only after you choose Accept analytics. Refusing leaves the demo, documentation and downloads available. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change or withdraw your choice at any time.

Your choice is stored in this browser for up to six months. Without JavaScript, no Analytics tag is loaded. This website setting does not configure the Miblo application.

What is measured

After acceptance, Google receives page views and interactions with the demo, installation command and download links, along with technical information such as browser language and screen size. Analytics uses a cookie identifier to distinguish visits. We configure these cookies to expire after six months without extending that period on each visit.

Only campaign labels such as utm_source=linkedin are retained from page query parameters. Search text, request bodies and clipboard contents are not sent by our tracking code. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in the site tag. The CLI itself is not instrumented by this website.

Who receives the data

The site is maintained by NaSuRall and hosted by Hostinger. Optional audience measurement uses Google Analytics. Google may process data outside your country; see Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

Withdrawal stops future measurement in this browser and removes the Analytics cookies accessible to the site. It does not automatically erase events already received by Google. For a question about this site, use the maintainer’s public contact channels; do not post personal information in a public issue.
